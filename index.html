Current product

Man-Overboard Detection and Crew-Location System

Our first system under development is designed to estimate crew-device locations aboard a vessel and help identify movement consistent with a potential man-overboard event. The prototype combines vessel-mounted positioning hardware, crew-worn devices, onboard software, and planned secondary detection methods.

Problem

A person entering the water may not be noticed immediately, especially during demanding vessel operations, limited visibility, or reduced staffing. Crews need dependable awareness tools that can support faster recognition and response without creating excessive false alarms.

Technology

The current prototype uses ultra-wideband positioning to measure distances between crew devices and fixed vessel reference points. Onboard software processes those measurements to estimate crew locations, while additional wireless detection methods are being evaluated as independent supporting layers.

Mission

Our mission is to develop practical marine technologies that improve situational awareness and support faster, more informed emergency response.

Development Status

The first prototype is undergoing hardware calibration, software development, controlled testing, and early vessel-based evaluation. The system is not commercially available, and its architecture and capabilities remain subject to continued development.

Future Vision

Marriner Marine Technologies aims to expand beyond its initial system into broader crew-location, vessel-safety, emergency-response, and search-and-rescue technologies for commercial fishing vessels, recreational boats, yachts, and larger maritime operations.
