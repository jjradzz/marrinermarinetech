MMT Sentinel

Man-overboard detection and crew-location system

In development

MMT Sentinel is being developed to give operators real-time awareness of crew aboard a vessel and help them recognize a potential man-overboard event. Real-time crew-location has been demonstrated through vessel testing and controlled validation.

The problem

A man-overboard event can happen without anyone seeing it, especially during active operations, in poor visibility, in noise, or with reduced staffing. Sentinel is being developed to provide an additional source of awareness on board when it matters most.

Who it's for

The first development effort is focused on commercial fishing vessels and their crews. Future versions may be adapted for recreational boats, yachts, workboats, cargo vessels, and other maritime operations.

Crew awareness

Each crew member wears a small device, and the operator sees their status in real time.

Faster alerts

Designed to flag a potential man-overboard event quickly, with audible and visual alerts on board.

Built for the deck

Developed with commercial fishing crews in mind and tested aboard a working vessel.

Planned features

Features remain subject to continued development and validation.

  • Real-time crew status
  • Position awareness aboard the vessel
  • Configurable vessel boundaries
  • Audible and visual alerts
  • Local touchscreen display
  • System-health monitoring
  • Event logging
  • Coverage for enclosed and below-deck areas

Testing so far

Sentinel has progressed from bench testing through an initial field test aboard a working commercial fishing vessel and a formal controlled validation session. A subsequent improvement was independently validated to substantially improve performance.

Read development updates

Availability

Sentinel is a research and development prototype. It is not available for purchase, and no commercial release date has been announced.

Common questions

What is Sentinel intended to do?

It is being developed to show where crew are aboard a vessel and provide an additional source of awareness for potential man-overboard events.

Can I buy Sentinel today?

No. The system remains under development and is not commercially available.

Has Sentinel been tested aboard a vessel?

Yes. Sentinel has completed an initial vessel field test and a formal controlled validation session, and a later improvement was independently validated. It has not yet completed the full vessel validation needed for commercial deployment.

Are you looking for testing or industry partners?

Yes. We welcome conversations with vessel operators, captains, fishermen, marine-safety professionals, researchers, and organizations interested in responsible prototype testing or collaboration.

Help shape Sentinel

We'd like to hear from operators who can share insight into vessel layouts, crew workflows, and emergency response.
